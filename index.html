OUR HERITAGE

A family bazaar,
since 1930.

What began as a humble corner shop has grown into the neighbourhood's most-loved super bazar. For over ninety years, the Palange family has stocked the shelves your family relies on — measured by the same promise of fair prices and a friendly face.
